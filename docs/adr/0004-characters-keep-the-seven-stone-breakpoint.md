# Characters keep the 7-stone breakpoint

Every creature's Encumbrance Level follows from its Normal Load. Humanoids reach Low Encumbrance at one and a half times their Normal Load, but characters reach it at 1.4 times. A character's levels are Normal Load, 1.4 × Normal Load, 2 × Normal Load, and 4 × Normal Load plus its Strength modifier. With the usual Normal Load of 5 stone that is 5, 7, 10 and 20 + STR stone (#281).

The game designer confirmed that characters are a special case. The 7-stone breakpoint has to stay so that the usual sets of armour and weapons give the speeds old-school D&D gives, and adventures stay compatible. The plain Humanoid rule would put it at 7½ stone.

## Considered Options

- **One Humanoid formula for everyone.** Simpler, but moves the character breakpoint to 7½ stone and changes the speed of common armour loadouts.
- **Fixed 5 / 7 / 10 / 20 + STR stone for characters.** Matches the book exactly but ignores Normal Load, so a house rule that changes a character's Normal Load (a strong race, say) could not change their levels.
- **Normal Load + 2 stone.** Also gives 7 at 5 stone, but doesn't scale with Normal Load the way every other level does.

## Consequences

- Encumbrance Levels are worked out three ways: for characters, for Humanoid monsters, and for other monsters.
- 1.4 is a factor chosen for the 5-stone case only. A character with another Normal Load gets a breakpoint the book never states.
